Services

Six trades, delivered by our own crews

Every scope below is delivered by our own directly employed crews using our own plant. Standards and quantities are stated on each service page.

Surface protection

The full surface protection cycle — stripping corrosion and old coating, preparing the substrate, and applying the specified system.

  • Grit blasting and sweep blasting
  • Bilge and tank cleaning and painting
  • De-rusting and painting
Scope and standards

Insulation & cladding

Supply, fabrication and installation of insulation systems on vessels and industrial plant, including removal and replacement of failed insulation.

  • Stud welding and insulation
  • Insulation and cladding on exhaust systems
  • Insulation beneath deck covering
Scope and standards

Deck covering & coating

Deck covering and coating systems on vessel decks, accommodation spaces and working areas — removal, preparation, build-up and finish.

  • Removal and deck plate preparation
  • Priming and covering compound
  • Levelling, screeding and finishing
  • Finish coating and non-slip systems
Scope and standards

Steel renewal & fabrication

Steel renewal on vessels and structures — cropping out wasted plate and sections, fabricating and fitting replacements — alongside general fabrication.

  • Steel renewal — cropping and replacement
  • Insert plates, doublers and section renewal
  • Replacement structural members
  • Stud welding and on-board repair welding

Cable tray installation

Tray and support installation through machinery spaces, accommodation and deck runs, coordinated with the yard electrical trades.

  • Tray, ladder and trunking runs
  • Supports and hangers
  • Penetration sealing
  • Coordinated with yard electricians
Scope and standards

Scaffolding, access & sheeting

Erection and dismantling of access scaffolding, with sheeting and encapsulation where the work inside it requires containment.

  • Scaffolding with cloth sheeting
  • Hull and dock-bottom access
  • Tank and confined-space staging
  • Erection and dismantling labour
