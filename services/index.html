Services

Insulation, coating, fabrication, cable tray and access

Every scope below is delivered by our own directly employed crews using our own plant. Standards and quantities are stated on each service page.

Thermal insulation

Pipework, valves, exhaust systems and engine-room lagging, with fabricated metal cladding.

  • Removable valve and flange jackets
  • Mineral wool and ceramic fibre
  • Aluminium / GI sheet cladding
  • 20 insulators, directly employed
Scope and standards

Surface protection

De-rusting, abrasive blasting and airless-spray protective coating to a specified surface standard and dry film thickness.

  • Abrasive blasting to SA 2.5
  • Airless spray application
  • Dry film thickness recorded per coat
  • 8 blasters and 8 painters
Scope and standards

Steel fabrication

Structural and plate work, seat and bracket fabrication, stud welding and on-board repair welding.

  • Three-phase welding to IFI standard
  • Stud welding
  • Seats, brackets and supports
  • 15 welders, directly employed
Scope and standards

Cable tray installation

Tray and support installation through machinery spaces, accommodation and deck runs, coordinated with the yard electrical trades.

  • Tray, ladder and trunking runs
  • Supports and hangers
  • Penetration sealing
  • Coordinated with yard electricians
Scope and standards

Scaffolding & access

Erection and dismantling of access scaffolding for hull, tank and machinery-space work.

  • Hull and dock-bottom access
  • Tank and confined-space staging
  • Erection and dismantling labour
  • 15 scaffolders, directly employed
