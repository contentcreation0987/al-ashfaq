Services / Surface protection

Grit blasting, de-rusting and protective painting

A protective coating is only as good as the surface beneath it. Most coating failures are preparation failures. Al-Ashfaq Enterprises delivers the full surface protection cycle — stripping corrosion and old coating, preparing the substrate, and applying the specified system.

Request a quotation
Scope

What we deliver

01

Grit blasting and sweep blasting

Full abrasive blasting where the substrate needs to be taken back to bare metal, and sweep blasting where an existing sound coating only needs a key and profile before overcoating. We select the approach against your specified preparation grade, not against what is quickest.

02

Bilge and tank cleaning and painting

Cleaning, preparation and coating of bilges, ballast tanks, void spaces and internal compartments. This is confined space work — it runs under the facility’s permit system, with forced ventilation, gas monitoring and continuous attendance outside the space.

03

De-rusting and painting

Manual and power-tool de-rusting for areas where abrasive blasting is not practical — around fitted equipment, in restricted access, or where containment cannot be established. Followed by the specified primer, intermediate and finish coats.

Standards

We prepare surfaces to the grade stated in your scope of work and apply coatings to the manufacturer’s data sheet — surface profile, substrate and ambient temperature, humidity, recoat windows and dry film thickness. Where inspection is required we work alongside your appointed coating inspector.
