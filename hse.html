HSE

Health, safety and worker welfare

We work inside the yard permit system. Our crews are directly employed, briefed on the scope before mobilisation, and covered by a group life insurance policy.

Commitments

What we hold ourselves to on site

  • Permit-controlled working
    Hot work, confined-space entry and working at height are carried out under the permits issued by the facility. We do not start a task outside its permit.
    Yard permit system
  • Confined-space ventilation
    Tank and double-bottom work is ventilated with our own blowers before and during entry, with an attendant posted at the access.
    6 blowers held
  • Personal protective equipment
    Blasting hoods and air-fed respirators for blasters, eye and face protection for welders, harnesses for work at height.
    Issued by us
  • Group life insurance
    Every Al-Ashfaq Enterprises employee is covered under a group life policy. Our tradesmen are employed, not engaged as casual labour per job.
    Jubilee Life Insurance
  • Briefed, familiar crews
    The same crews return to the same yards, so they know the site rules, the access routes and the escalation contacts.
    66 personnel
  • Documentation on request
    Method statements, risk assessments and insurance documentation are issued against a tender reference.
    Pre-qualification pack

We do not claim third-party HSE certification. If your tender requires a specific certification or safety statistic, contact us and we will state exactly what we hold rather than imply more.
