Who we are

A contractor, not a labour supplier

For 28 years we have worked on ship repair and shipbuilding projects for Karachi Shipyard & Engineering Works and the Pakistan Navy Dockyard, across multiple departments at both facilities. We have delivered over 300 projects in that time.

We take the docking from first surface preparation to final coat: grit blasting of underwater hull and decks, tank and bilge cleaning, de-rusting and painting, access scaffolding, steel renewal and fabrication, and then stud welding, insulation, exhaust cladding, cable tray and deck anti-skid work as the job closes out.

We work with a directly employed workforce of 66 tradesmen and our own plant. We do not sublet our core trades, and we do not hire in crews per job. The same blasters, painters, scaffolders, welders and insulators return to site project after project — which is why the yards keep calling us back.

How we work

From scope to signed handover

  1. Step 01

    Site survey

    We attend the yard, measure the scope and confirm access and permit requirements.

    Same working day, West Wharf

  2. Step 02

    Method & quotation

    Written method statement with the standards referenced, priced by area or linear metre.

  3. Step 03

    Mobilisation

    Our own crews and plant on site — compressors, blast pots, spray units and welding sets.

  4. Step 04

    Inspection & handover

    Readings recorded per coat and signed off with the yard inspector before handover.
